Frequently asked questions

How do I log a drink?

Tap a category under Quick add on Today, or open Log. Choose a UK preset where available, or enter your own drink. Set the serving size in millilitres and the total sugars in grams for that serving. Check the time and tap Save drink.

What if the sugar amount on my pack is different?

Use the pack’s “of which sugars” figure for the amount you drank. If the label gives grams per 100ml, multiply by the volume you drank in millilitres and divide by 100. You can type the result into the sugar field. A preset adjusts automatically when you change the serving size until you enter a different sugar amount. Tap Use label amount to restore the preset calculation.

How do I edit or delete a drink?

Open a recent drink on Today, or tap See all and choose a row in Drink history. Tap Save changes after editing, or tap Delete drink and confirm.

How do I set a goal or see my average?

In Settings, enter your own drinks-only goal in grams and tap Save goal. Use Remove goal to clear it. In Trends, select 7 days, 30 days or 3 months. The daily average uses the days you logged at least one drink.

Can I use SwigCount offline or on another iPhone?

Logging works offline. To sync your log and goal, use the same Apple Account and enable iCloud for SwigCount on both iPhones. Give both devices time to sync while online.

Contact support

For help or feedback, email SwigCount support. For a problem, include your iPhone model, iOS version and the steps you took. A screenshot is optional. Remove any personal information before sending it.
