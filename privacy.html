SwigCount Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

SwigCount is an iPhone app for logging the total sugars in your drinks. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” mean Thomas Williams, the developer based in the United Kingdom and the controller of personal information we receive through support and privacy enquiries.

1. Drink records and goals

SwigCount saves your drink name, category, serving size, total sugars, recorded date and time, and any preset used. It also saves your optional daily goal and the settings needed to manage it. Each saved record has an internal identifier. The app uses these records to display your log, calculate totals and trends, and show progress towards your goal. You can use SwigCount offline without creating a SwigCount account.

2. Private iCloud sync

If your iPhone is signed in to iCloud and iCloud is available to SwigCount, the app uses Apple’s CloudKit service to sync your drinks and goal to the private iCloud database associated with your Apple Account. Sync lets your compatible devices using the same account share your log. We cannot access your private iCloud records through our developer account. Apple handles iCloud and associated service information under its Privacy Policy and iCloud terms. You can manage iCloud access in your iPhone settings.

3. Retention and deletion

SwigCount keeps your saved log until you delete entries or remove the app and its local data. To delete a drink, open it from Today or Drink history, tap Delete drink and confirm. To clear your goal, open Settings and tap Remove goal. Changes may take time to reach other devices using iCloud. SwigCount has no separate account or developer-held copy of your log for us to delete.

Deleting the app from one device does not necessarily remove data from iCloud, other devices or backups. Delete entries and remove your goal before uninstalling if you want those changes to sync. You can manage iCloud storage and backups separately in your Apple Account settings. Apple manages retention of its backups and service records under its own terms.

4. Analytics, advertising and external links

SwigCount has no advertising, tracking, or analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not sell your drink records or share them with advertisers. Links to NHS guidance and UK product sources open external pages. When you follow a link, that website handles your visit under its own privacy policy. SwigCount does not send your drink log with the link.

5. Support and privacy enquiries

If you contact us, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments or other details you choose to send. We use this information to respond, provide support and handle follow-up. Our lawful basis for ordinary support under UK data protection law is our legitimate interest in supporting SwigCount and answering enquiries. Where the law requires us to act, such as on an applicable data protection request, we rely on that legal obligation.

We keep correspondence while your enquiry is open and for as long as reasonably necessary for related follow-up, dispute resolution or a legal obligation. We delete it when it is no longer needed. Please avoid sending passwords or sensitive information.

We use Gmail to receive, store and reply to messages. Google handles information under its Privacy Policy and describes applicable safeguards for international transfers in its data transfer information. We do not use support messages for marketing or automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

6. Visiting this website

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub receives technical information, including IP addresses and request information, to provide and secure the service. Its Privacy Statement explains how it handles, retains and transfers that information. This website uses no advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, remote fonts or cookies set by us.

7. Your rights and contact details

Depending on applicable law, you may be able to ask for access to, correction or deletion of information we hold, ask us to restrict its use, or receive it in a portable form. You can object to processing based on our legitimate interests. We can respond to requests about correspondence you send us. Your iPhone and iCloud controls let you manage your drink records.

For privacy questions or requests, email meteorapp.support@gmail.com. In the UK, you can also contact the Information Commissioner’s Office.

8. Children and changes to this policy

SwigCount is a general-purpose drink log. It does not ask for an age or require an account. We may update this policy when the app or our practices change. This page will show the date of the latest update.
